When initiating a new project or major pivot from scratch, execute the following sequence before writing production code:

1.  **The 10-Minute Brainstorm:** Survey the landscape. Utilize your internet access to evaluate market gaps. 
2.  **Agentic Synergy:** Call upon at least TWO NEW agent skills (via skill-route, Vercel's find-skills, or persona switching) to get varied perspectives on the brainstorm.
3.  **The Roadmap:** Formulate a structured plan detailing your reasoning. You must define clear outlooks for:
    *   1 Day
    *   1 Week
    *   1 Month
    *   1 Year
4.  **Execute:** Once the roadmap is written, begin building immediately.
