# Product Hunt Launch — TradeDesk

## Headline
TradeDesk — Business management for trade professionals

## Tagline
Invoicing, field service, and compliance — all in one simple tool built for trade businesses

## Description
TradeDesk is an all-in-one business management app built specifically for trade professionals — plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, landscapers, and other field service businesses.

**What makes us different:**
- **Built for trades, not generic businesses** — every feature maps to how trade work actually happens
- **Invoicing with automated reminders** — set up once, get paid 40% faster
- **Field service scheduling** — assign jobs, track status, convert estimates to invoices
- **Compliance tracking** — never let a license or insurance certificate expire again
- **Modular pricing** — pay only for what you need, or use the free tier for up to 5 invoices/month

**Key features:**
- Customer management with full history
- Create and send professional invoices
- Automated invoice reminders (email + push)
- Field service job scheduling and tracking
- Quote/estimate builder with approval workflow
- Expense tracking by job and category
- Compliance document tracking with expiry alerts
- Monthly reports: revenue, expenses, job profitability
- Referral program that gives both sides a free month

**Pricing:**
- Free: 5 invoices/mo, 10 customers, unlimited quotes
- Invoice Reminders: $19/mo
- Compliance Tracking: $49/mo
- Field Service: $15/mo

**Launch offer:** 30% off annual plans with code **PHLAUNCH**
